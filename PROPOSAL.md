# Lyntrix Learn — Next-Gen Academy & Multi-Master LMS Platform Proposal

> **Executive Business & SaaS Proposal for Sri Lankan Tuition Masters, Institute Owners, and Academies.**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary (සාරාංශය)

Lyntrix Learn is Sri Lanka’s premier Multi-Tenant Tuition LMS & Academy Management Platform designed specifically to help Tuition Masters build a high-revenue digital brand.

By providing **custom branded subdomains**, **anti-piracy video playback**, **automated bank slip verification queues**, and **laser QR attendance terminals**, Lyntrix Learn eliminates video piracy losses and operational hassles, allowing masters to scale their student base effortlessly.

## 🎯 Primary Challenges & Technical Solutions (ගුරුතුමන්ලාගේ අභියෝග සහ අපගේ විසඳුම්)

### 1. Video Piracy & Telegram Leaks (වීඩියෝ පිටපත් වීම වැළැක්වීම)
- **Problem**: Traditional platforms allow students to screen-record or share video links via Telegram & WhatsApp.
- **Solution**: **Anti-Piracy Moving Watermark Cinema Player**. A semi-transparent watermark containing the logged-in student's **Full Name, Index Number, and Mobile Number** moves dynamically across the video screen continuously.

### 2. Manual Bank Slip Verification Delay (බැංකු රිසිට්පත් අතින් පරීක්ෂා කිරීමේ ප්‍රමාදය)
- **Problem**: Admins spend hundreds of hours manually verifying bank deposit/CDM slips every month.
- **Solution**: **Automated Bank Slip Queue & Online Card Gateway**. 1-click approval queue with reference checking that automatically grants instant access to monthly Zoom live classes and recordings.

### 3. Physical Hall Attendance Tracking (පන්ති ශාලා පැමිණීම සටහන් කිරීම)
- **Problem**: Long queues at physical hall doors during monthly fee card checking.
- **Solution**: **High-Speed Laser QR Scanner Terminal**. Scan physical hall students in under 1 second using a smartphone or USB barcode scanner with instant monthly fee verification.

### 4. Lack of Personal Branding (තමන්ගේම නමින් වෙබ් අඩවියක් නොමැතිකම)
- **Problem**: Being listed alongside competitors on shared course websites.
- **Solution**: **Custom Subdomain** (e.g. `https://kasunmaths.dilnethmadushanka.online`). Students see only your branded academy, courses, and schedules.
